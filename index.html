Coach Me If You Can

A career-coaching chatbot designed to help job seekers practice interviews, strengthen authentic résumé content, and plan their professional growth.

Introduction & description

Built for an AIML-500 AI Lab, this working assistant offers guided career-preparation conversations rather than one-size-fits-all advice.

Objective

Make career preparation more accessible through immediate, constructive practice and realistic next steps.

Process

Defined user needs, planned three coaching workflows, configured Chatbase instructions and a knowledge source, tested responses, corrected naming inconsistencies, and published a public Help page.

Tools & technologies

Chatbase, generative AI, prompt design, structured testing, and web-based deployment.

Value proposition & unique value

Combines practical career guidance with deliberate safeguards: no fabricated qualifications, no employment guarantees, and prompts to remove sensitive résumé information.

Relevance to my audience

Shows data science and AI engineering audiences how I can identify a user problem, configure a working LLM-based application, evaluate its behavior, and improve it based on test results. This is a no-code application, not a custom-trained ML model.
